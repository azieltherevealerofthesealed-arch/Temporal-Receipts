# Cryptography

Temporal Receipts use cryptography only for **integrity**, not authenticity.

## What Hashing Guarantees
- Detection of tampering
- Verification of sequence
- Lineage continuity

## What It Does NOT Guarantee
- Identity verification
- Authenticity of sources
- Truth of claims
- Resistance to Sybil attacks

Hashing is intentionally minimal.
This system favors transparency over enforcement.
